The Model — Points-Based Economy

Members bring it.We grade it.Points buy it.

No flat-rate buffet. No thrift bin. Garments are graded A, B, or C by brand and condition. Members earn points on intake and spend them like currency on what's currently in the room.

The Founder

Emily Petroff didn't want a clothing store. She wanted a room.

She started in 2023 in 1,400 square feet on Montana Avenue. The first members were the women in her own group chat. The second members were their sisters. By month four, the door was wedged open most afternoons because the bell could not keep up.

Three years later, the room has processed 319,799+ garments and built the only operating template the franchise will ever issue. Emily still works the floor on Wednesdays. The franchise programme is not an exit — it is an invitation.

I did not set out to build a franchise. I built a room I wanted to be in, and other women asked how to bring it home.

Frequently Asked

The detail.

If a question isn't answered here, it's answered on the call. We don't keep secrets from prospective founders — we keep them from competitors.

How is territory defined and protected?
Each territory is a protected radius — typically 8 miles in metro areas, 25 miles in secondary markets — drawn around your trade area. No other Switch Society opens inside that perimeter for the duration of your agreement. Markets above 350,000 households support multi-unit awards.
What is the royalty structure?
Royalty is 6% of gross membership and ancillary revenue, paid monthly. A 1.5% marketing fund contribution is reinvested into national brand programmes. Founder-rate territories lock these terms for the full initial 10-year term.
How long is the franchise term?
A 10-year initial term, with two 5-year renewal options at the prevailing renewal fee. The model is designed for compounding tenure — most franchise economics get better in years three through seven, not worse.
What real estate works for a Society?
1,200–1,800 sqft in a walkable neighbourhood retail block, ground floor, with natural light and back-of-house large enough to support intake, steaming, and the SWAP Party room. We approve every site.
What financing options exist?
Switch Society is on the SBA Franchise Registry. Most founders finance 50–70% of the project through SBA 7(a). We maintain relationships with three preferred lenders and will introduce you after FDD review.
What does training include?
Two weeks of in-person training in Billings: floor and intake, garment grading, SWAP Party hosting, the points platform, and the founding-member acquisition playbook. A regional coach is in your room for the first ninety days post-opening.
How do members earn and spend points?
Members deposit garments at intake; pieces are graded A, B, or C and points are issued accordingly. Points are spent like currency on any other member's items in the room. Monthly subscriptions add a baseline allotment — the floor that keeps the system liquid.
Do I need fashion or retail experience?
It helps but isn't required. The non-negotiable is taste — a clear point of view on what belongs in the room. We've awarded territories to a former hotelier, a buyer, and a personal stylist, and they have all opened strong.
Can I be a passive owner?
No. Switch Society is an owner-operator model. The room is a relationship business — members notice when the founder is in it and notice harder when she isn't.
